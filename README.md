# Skim

Skim is a phone app that uses AI to quickly turn long voice messages into text and summaries. It's made for young people and busy professionals who can't always listen to lengthy voice notes. Skim works with apps like WhatsApp and iMessage, automatically pulling out important details, dates, and to-dos. It turns messy audio into clear bullet points, saving people about 2 hours of listening each week.

See [overview.md](overview.md) for the key features.
